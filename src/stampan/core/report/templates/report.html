Colour. Measured on linear scanner RGB and converted to CIELAB D50 (2°) through the reference's characterisation of an IT8.7/2 target; the reference's cross-validated ΔE00 above is the accuracy to expect. L*, C* and h° are CIE LCh(ab). Pictures are rendered from the same colour model; on screen or paper they are only an approximation.

Perforation. Gauge in teeth per 2 cm, measured per side from the tooth profile and given to the nearest quarter. a × b is top/bottom × left/right; a/b means two opposite sides differ. Values marked * or “by hand” were set by the user; the measured value is kept on record. Low-confidence results are flagged.

Cancellation. Type and date as recorded by the user. “c.” marks a date that was estimated rather than read from a legible postmark.

Numbers. Stamp numbers are per sheet and are the same on the sheet pictures, in the charts and on the stamp cards.
